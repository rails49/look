# On a narrow window the rail becomes a strip

At a window width of `NARROW_WIDTH_PX` or less, the rail lies down as the strip along the top of the work pane, as it does at a window height of `TURN_HEIGHT_PX` or less. This holds for every view of every UI. On an upright phone the strip costs about 50px of height, where the rail costs a fifth of the width, and the views keep the same icons in the same order at every size. Two options were turned down (rails49/look#31). Moving the views into the gear when a view has no commands would have changed occupancy's live and diagnostics views and every dccex view, which never asked for it; dccex has no gear; and in control the views would move between the rail and the gear on switching from Run to Throttle. Letting one view opt out of the rail when narrow would have kept that move for the throttle and added a second way to reach the views.
